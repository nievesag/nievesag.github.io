about me

Last-year student of videogame development equipped with a deep love for videogames eager to start working in the game industry and make my mark.
By participating in game jams and working on both university and personal projects, I have gained experience working with and coordinating development teams, holding roles as a gameplay programmer, UI/UX programmer, game-flow programmer and engine programmer.

With a solid foundation in artistic knowledge, thanks to my experience as a visual development artist, painter and illustrator, I have been able to develop as a technical artist allowing me to work as a bridge between programmers and artists in development teams.

I approach programming as a creative tool for bringing worlds to life through my code.
